## 1. About the Internship

### 1.1 What is the Vicharanashala internship?
A two-month internship run by Vicharanashala, a research lab at IIT Ropar. You will work on a real open-source project under a mentor, after a short training phase tailored to where you already are. The internship is free — we do not charge, and the work is real.

### 1.2 What is VINS?
**VINS** is the Vicharanashala Internship — an online programme open to anyone who clears our interview. The work is real open-source contribution under a mentor, the certificate is from the Vicharanashala Lab for Education Design at IIT Ropar, and the programme itself is free (we charge nothing). There is no stipend.

If you are seeing a yellow VINS panel on your result page, you are selected.

### 1.3 What are the phases of VINS, and what do the badges mean?
VINS is structured as four phases. Each one is marked by a badge — a small token of where you are in the journey.

- 🥉 **Bronze (Phase 1)** — a short training period at the start, planned around what you already know. If you arrive already comfortable with the basics, your mentor may skip Bronze and put you straight on to the project.
- 🥈 **Silver (Phase 2)** — the main work. You contribute to a real open-source project under a Vicharanashala mentor. Finishing Bronze and Silver completes your internship and earns the certificate.
- 🥇 **Gold (Phase 3)** — a recognition awarded during Silver if your contribution stands on its own as a meaningful feature, not just a small fix.
- 🏆 **Platinum (Phase 4)** — a standing invitation to come back and visit the lab — a short trip — any time during the year after your internship ends. We help with travel through a small visit stipend.

Most interns finish at Bronze + Silver, and that is exactly what the certificate is for. Gold and Platinum are extras you can pick up if your work makes the case for them.

### 1.4 Who is the internship for? Are alumni eligible?
The internship is for **currently-enrolled students** at any college or university — undergraduate, postgraduate, or doctoral.

**Candidates who have already graduated and are not currently enrolled in any programme are not eligible** for this cycle. If you re-enrol later (higher studies, etc.), you are very welcome to apply again in a future cycle.

### 1.5 Is this the same as IIT Ropar's official Summer Research Internship?
No. Summership 2026 is a VLED Lab initiative. The certificate is issued by the **Vicharanashala Lab for Education Design**, not centrally by the institute. IIT Ropar runs a separate institutional summer research internship through its own office. Do not represent Summership 2026 as equivalent to that programme.

### 1.6 I have to attend my class tomorrow/today/some day — can I take leave?
Leave is not permitted. If you are also attending classes or exams, you will be relieved from the internship immediately and will need to join the next batch when it starts.

## 2. Timing and Dates

### 2.1 When can I start?
You can start any time in 2026 — VINS is flexible on the start date — **but there are two things you must hold in mind together**, and one strong recommendation.

**The hard rule.** Your internship must **finish by 31 December 2026.** That date is non-negotiable. Whatever start you pick, your end date (start + 2 months, with up to 1 month grace) must land on or before 31 December 2026.

**The strong recommendation: start as soon as possible.** The earlier you join, the more of the May–July main cohort you catch — and three things make starting earlier materially better:
- **Cohort networking.** The batch goes through Bronze together — peer discussions, parallel problem-solving, and lasting connections happen during this window.
- **TA support is concentrated in May–July.** TAs are full-time during this window. After this they return to their own college work and bandwidth is materially thinner.
- **Training rolls out with the cohort**, not piecemeal — you get the material with the discussion around it, not as a static document.

### 2.2 How long is the internship?
**Two months from your chosen start date**, with an optional **one-month grace period** if you need it. End must land on or before 31 December 2026.

### 2.3 Can I start in July, August or later if I have exams now?
Yes — but only if your exams genuinely make an earlier start impossible. Wait until your exams are done, then opt in and start. Do not attempt to juggle this internship with ongoing exams. Make sure your chosen start date plus 2 months (or 3 with grace) lands on or before 31 December 2026.

### 2.4 Can I start with the cohort and take a relaxation during my exam window?
No. This is not an arrangement we offer.

VINS is a full-attention internship — six to ten hours a day, sometimes more. Splitting that with college exams damages both sides. If your exams fall inside the cohort duration, defer your start to after your exams end.

**A note on consequences.** If we later learn that a candidate was sitting college exams during their internship period, **we reserve the right to terminate the internship or withhold the certificate at any time** — including after the internship has otherwise been completed.

### 2.5 Can I take leave or get an exemption during the internship for an exam scheduled in June?
The attendance rule is firm — the 55-day continuous window is a non-negotiable part of the internship, and we cannot offer an exemption for an exam during this period.

### 2.6 Are orientation session recordings shared with interns, and can project or group assignments be changed after watching them?
Recordings of the sessions will not be provided. However, we may provide access to an abridged version of a talk or session if we consider it important. We do not guarantee this for every session.

## 3. NOC (No Objection Certificate)

### 3.1 What dates do I put on the NOC?
- **Default:** **your chosen start date → your start + 2 months** (with up to 1 month grace), ensuring the end date is on or before 31 December 2026. Pick the earliest start date you can realistically make — the May–July summer window is the main cohort.
- If the NOC will be signed on a specific later date, pick a start date *after* the signature date.

### 3.2 Who can sign the NOC?
Any authorised signatory at your college: HOD, Acting HOD (during holidays), Principal, Dean, Director, or Training & Placement Officer. For dual-degree students, either institution can sign — pick whichever is easier. For IITM BS Online Degree (standalone) students, any officer from the BS office can sign.

### 3.3 When do I submit the NOC? Is the deadline hard?
The deadline is **not hard** — there is no specific cut-off date. **But for the internship to actually begin properly, you should submit it as early as possible and join the current summer cohort.** We strongly do not recommend starting later in the year.

### 3.4 What format should I use? Do I need to design it myself?
No — **we provide a printable NOC format**. Once your result is out and you log in to samagama.in, you will see a **Download blank NOC** button on your dashboard. Take a printout, get it physically signed and stamped by your authorised signatory, scan it, and upload the signed PDF. You do not need to draft anything yourself, and you do **not** need college letterhead.

### 3.5 What if my college / Program Chair gives me an NOC in their own format?
A college's own NOC format is acceptable, as long as all four of the required entries are present:
- The signing authority's (HOD / Dean / Program Chair / Principal) **handwritten signature**
- The signing authority's **official email address** (we cross-check to verify the signature is genuine)
- Your **full name**, and
- Your **signature**

### 3.6 Does it need to be signed by hand?
Yes. Three things are required:
- The authorised signatory's **handwritten signature**
- The **institutional rubber stamp / seal** applied in the designated area
- The signatory's **email address** filled in the designated field — we automatically cross-check to verify the signature is genuine.

Digital signatures are not accepted on the PDF path.

### 3.7 Can my HOD email the NOC instead of signing a printout?
**Yes — there is a fully-equivalent email-forward path.**

How it works:
1. From your dashboard, download the **text NOC** (the "⬇ Download text NOC (email path)" button).
2. Fill in the student-side fields, then email the file to your HOD and ask them to forward it.
3. **Your HOD forwards the email to `sudarshan@iitrpr.ac.in` from their official institutional email address**, with the subject line: `NOC for my student <Your Full Name>`

Two non-negotiable conditions:
- The forward must come from the HOD's **official institutional email address** (college/university domain) — not a personal Gmail or Outlook account.
- The subject line must start with **`NOC for my student`** so it routes correctly.

### 3.8 How do I download and upload the NOC?
Both happen on your **dashboard** at samagama.in once your result is out. The two buttons:
- **Download blank NOC** — saves the printable NOC format PDF.
- **Upload signed NOC (PDF)** — opens a file picker; the file must be a PDF of at most 1 MB.

### 3.9 What if my NOC is not formally verified?
NOC verification takes time — typically anywhere between an hour and one full working day from the moment you upload.

If you are planning to start in the coming days and need your offer letter sooner, there is a tentative route: upload a **self-declaration** on your profile and a **tentative offer letter** will be issued to you immediately. The formal offer letter follows once your NOC clears verification.

### 3.10 My online course (Masai, NPTEL, Coursera, etc.) won't issue an NOC. What do I do?
The internship is open only to candidates currently enrolled in a **full-time degree programme** at a recognised college or university. Online-only courses — Masai Institute, NPTEL/MOOC enrolments, Coursera, Udacity, bootcamps, and similar — do not by themselves make a candidate eligible.

### 3.11 My HOD/college official wants written confirmation before signing my NOC. What do I show them?
Use the **tentative offer letter** route:
1. Log in to samagama.in and open your profile.
2. Upload a brief **self-declaration**.
3. A **tentative offer letter** on Vicharanashala letterhead is issued to your dashboard immediately.
4. Hand the tentative offer letter to your HOD/college official — it serves as the written confirmation they need to sign your NOC.

### 3.12 Can Prof. Sudarshan Iyengar or a faculty member from IIT Ropar sign my NOC?
No. Your NOC must be signed by an authorised signatory at the institution where you are enrolled as a student. Sudarshan Iyengar is a faculty member at IIT Ropar and cannot sign your NOC in a personal capacity. An online-only certification course (even if offered jointly with an IIT) does not meet the eligibility requirement on its own.

## 4. Selection, Offer Letter, and Certificate

### 4.1 How do I know I am selected?
If you can see your yellow VINS result panel on samagama.in, you are selected. There is no separate selection step or confirmation email.

### 4.2 How do I opt into VINS?
Tell Yaksha in the chat: *"I want to take up the online internship without stipend."* Yaksha will confirm. Opting in **is** the selection — no separate confirmation email is sent at that stage.

### 4.3 When do I get the offer letter?
There are two paths:

**Path 1 — Formal offer letter (default).** Issued once both of these are in place:
1. Your signed NOC has been verified (typically between an hour and one full working day after upload).
2. You have confirmed your internship start and end dates on the dashboard.

**Path 2 — Tentative offer letter (faster).** Upload a **self-declaration** on your profile instead. A **tentative offer letter** will be issued immediately, and the formal offer letter follows once your NOC clears verification.

**The offer letter lives on your dashboard at samagama.in, not in your email.** When it is issued you will receive a notification email from `no-reply@vicharanashala.ai` with the subject *"Your Vicharanashala Offer Letter is ready — please log in to download."*

### 4.4 Will I get a certificate?
**Yes — every intern who completes the internship gets a certificate** from Vicharanashala, IIT Ropar. Candidates who drop out mid-way do not get a certificate.

### 4.5 How do I confirm my internship dates?
Log in to samagama.in. On the dashboard, you will see a yellow card titled **"🗓️ Confirm your internship dates"**. The two date pickers pre-fill with sensible defaults for the current cohort. Your end must be on or before **31 December 2026**.

**Order doesn't matter.** You can save your dates **before or after** uploading your NOC — both paths work.

### 4.6 I am a minor/major in AI student at IIT Ropar — can I join?
Minor/Major in AI course from IIT Ropar is a certification course and there will be a different track of internship equivalent to them. Kindly write to us separately for this. You should be a registered student in a UG/PG programme with some university.

### 4.7 How do I accept the offer letter?
**Reply to the offer-letter email** from `no-reply@vicharanashala.ai`. In the body, paste the following acceptance statement **exactly as printed**, with your full name inserted and a date added:

> I, [Full Name], confirm that I have read, understood, and accepted all terms, conditions, and obligations set out in this offer letter and in the program FAQ at samagama.in. I formally accept the offer of Summer Internship 2026.

**Copy-paste this sentence as-is.** Do not paraphrase. The reply must reach us within **5 days** of the offer letter being sent. Use Reply All.

### 4.8 What if I reply without using the exact acceptance format?
The offer is **withdrawn**, effective immediately, with no further correspondence. The withdrawal is final. Non-compliant examples include paraphrasing, a bare "I accept", missing the date, or missing the FAQ-reference clause.

### 4.9 I received a withdrawal email because I didn't accept the offer letter correctly. Can it be reversed?
Once withdrawn, the offer cannot be reversed. Please read the FAQ carefully and follow the process precisely.

### 4.10 What happens after I send my acceptance? My dashboard doesn't update.
The dashboard does not display a live acceptance status. Once your correctly formatted reply is received, processing happens on the admin side. You will receive a follow-up communication. Allow 24–48 hours.

### 4.11 How do I change my internship dates *before* the offer letter is issued?
Edit the dates on the **"Confirm your internship dates"** card on your dashboard anytime before the offer letter is generated.

### 4.12 When and how do I get the Zoom link for the kickoff meeting?
The Zoom link for the kickoff meeting (orientation) is sent via email to your registered email address, typically 24–48 hours before the meeting. Check your spam/promotions folder if you don't see it.

### 4.13 How to proceed if NOC is not available and my chosen internship start date is approaching?
Upload a self-declaration on your profile to receive a tentative offer letter immediately. Pursue your NOC in parallel and upload it as soon as it is available. The formal offer letter will be issued automatically once the NOC is verified.

### 4.14 When does my internship actually begin? Will I receive a notification on the day?
Your internship begins on your confirmed start date. There is no automated "start day" notification — your dashboard will reflect your active internship status. You are expected to be ready and working from that date.

### 4.15 Can I switch from VINS (online) to VISE (offline) after being selected?
No. Once selected for VINS, switching to VISE (or vice versa) is not permitted for this cycle.

### 4.16 Can I change my internship dates *after* the offer letter has been issued?
Date changes after offer letter issuance require a fresh NOC matching the new dates and admin approval. Reach out via Yaksha chat on your dashboard.

## 9. Rosetta — Your Internship Journal

### 9.1 What is Rosetta?
Rosetta is your personal internship journal — a structured, daily reflective log you maintain throughout the internship. It is named after the Rosetta Stone: something that helps translate experience into understanding.

### 9.2 Why does this exist? Is it just busywork?
It is not busywork. The thinking routines embedded in Rosetta are a core part of the Vicharanashala pedagogy. Consistent reflection accelerates learning, improves problem-solving, and makes your work legible — to yourself first, and to your mentor.

### 9.3 What is a "thinking routine"?
A short, structured prompt that directs your attention to a specific aspect of your learning or work — for example, *what surprised me today*, *what I would do differently*, or *what I still don't understand*. Think of it as cognitive scaffolding.

### 9.4 How do I get my Rosetta journal?
Your Rosetta journal is provided to you as part of your onboarding materials, shared at or shortly after the orientation/kickoff session.

### 9.5 How do I use it day to day?
At the end of each working day, open your journal and respond to the day's prompts. Keep it brief and honest. The point is consistent, genuine reflection — not a polished essay.

### 9.6 How long should each entry be?
There is no minimum or maximum. Write enough to genuinely address the prompt — typically a few sentences to a short paragraph per prompt. Quality of reflection matters more than length.

### 9.7 What is the one rule?
Write it yourself. No AI tools, no copy-paste from the internet, no asking a friend. The journal is only useful if it represents your actual thinking.

### 9.8 Can I use ChatGPT or any AI tool to write my entries?
No. See §9.7. Using AI to write your journal entries defeats the entire purpose and is a violation of the internship's academic integrity expectations.

### 9.9 What if I miss a day?
Write a brief catch-up entry noting that you missed a day and why. Patterns of missed days without explanation are noted and may affect your evaluation.

### 9.10 Will anyone read my journal during the internship?
Your mentor may review your journal periodically. It is one of the signals they use to understand how you are progressing and where you need support.

### 9.11 Can the prompts change mid-internship?
Yes. Prompts may be updated to reflect the phase of the internship or to address emerging themes in the cohort's work. Updates will be communicated.

### 9.12 How do I submit Rosetta at the end?
Submission instructions are shared as part of the end-of-internship checklist. The journal is typically submitted digitally via your dashboard.

### 9.13 I have a question about Rosetta that is not answered here. What do I do?
Ask via Yaksha chat on your samagama.in dashboard.

### 9.14 My college requires a written confirmation that the internship is self-paced and will not clash with college classes — what document can I share with them?
The offer letter (tentative or formal) on Vicharanashala letterhead serves as the official documentation of the internship. It describes the programme. If your college needs additional assurance, you may also point them to the public programme page at samagama.in/internship.

## 12. ViBe Platform

### 12.1 How do I log in to ViBe?
Use the invite link sent to your registered email. Log in with the credentials you set during registration. Do not create a new account manually — use the invite link.

### 12.2 Invite accepted but shows "No course enrolled"?
This is usually a propagation delay. Wait 15–30 minutes and refresh. If it persists after an hour, raise it via Yaksha chat with your registered email and the invite link you used.

### 12.3 Why are videos stuck or repeating?
See §12.5 for a full troubleshooting guide. Common causes: switching tabs (which the proctoring system detects), browser compatibility issues, or slow internet.

### 12.4 Can I use a mobile or tablet?
ViBe is designed for desktop/laptop use. Mobile and tablet use is not recommended and may cause tracking or proctoring issues.

### 12.5 I'm experiencing video issues (stuck, looping, skipping) on ViBe. How do I troubleshoot?
Try in order:
1. Stay on the ViBe tab — do not switch tabs or minimise the window.
2. Use Chrome or Firefox on a desktop/laptop.
3. Clear browser cache, then reload.
4. Check your internet connection stability.
5. Disable browser extensions (especially ad-blockers or privacy extensions).
6. If the issue persists, use the **Flag** option on ViBe to report the specific video/quiz, or raise via Yaksha chat.

### 12.6 I have completed all videos and quizzes in the ViBe course, but my progress is still showing less than 100%. What should I do?
Progress updates can lag. Wait a few hours and refresh. If still not resolved, use the **Flag** option on ViBe or raise via Yaksha chat with your registered email and a screenshot.

### 12.7 I feel the ViBe content or platform is not good or I am unhappy with the way progress is evaluated. Can I request an exception or bypass the system?
No exceptions or bypasses are available. Feedback on content quality can be submitted via the Flag option.

### 12.8 Is the ViBe consent form compulsory? What if I don't want to grant camera access?
The consent form is compulsory for participation in the ViBe course as part of this internship. Camera access is required for the proctoring system.

### 12.9 What are penalty scores on the ViBe platform, and how do they affect our performance or HP?
Penalty scores are issued when the proctoring system detects behaviour that violates learning integrity rules (tab-switching, multiple faces, no face detected, etc.). They are factored into your Phase 1 evaluation. Minimise penalties by following the study environment guidelines in §12.26.

### 12.10 When should I use the Flag option on ViBe, and when should I contact support?
Use **Flag** for content issues (incorrect questions, broken videos, platform bugs). Contact support via Yaksha chat for account issues, access problems, or anything that requires a human to intervene.

### 12.11 What is Linear Progression on ViBe?
Linear Progression means you must complete each video/quiz in sequence before the next one unlocks. You cannot skip ahead.

### 12.12 Can I use the left navigation panel to jump ahead to a later video or quiz?
No. Under Linear Progression, the navigation panel shows you where you are but does not allow jumping ahead to locked content.

### 12.13 I am seeing a red "Access Restricted" banner. Is this a bug?
No — it is intentional. See §12.14.

### 12.14 How do I resolve the "Access Restricted" error?
"Access Restricted" appears when you attempt to access content before completing the prerequisite. Complete the prior video and/or quiz fully (including achieving the pass threshold if a quiz is involved), then proceed. If you believe you have completed the prerequisite and the banner persists, use the Flag option or Yaksha chat.

### 12.15 Why does ViBe sometimes make me re-watch a clip after a quiz?
If you score below the pass threshold on a quiz, the system loops you back to the relevant video section so you can review the material before re-attempting the quiz. This is by design.

### 12.16 What kinds of quiz questions will I see on ViBe?
Multiple-choice, true/false, and occasionally short conceptual questions embedded between video segments. Questions are drawn directly from the video content.

### 12.17 Are the same proctoring rules applied to every course on ViBe?
Proctoring rules may vary by course. The rules applicable to the AI Fundamentals course used in this internship are communicated at the start of Phase 1 and are consistent throughout that course.

### 12.18 What does the "quiet helper" on ViBe actually do?
The quiet helper is a low-visibility overlay that gives you real-time, unobtrusive feedback when the proctoring system detects a potential issue (e.g., face not visible). It nudges you to correct the issue before a penalty is logged.

### 12.19 Does ViBe record long videos of me while I'm learning?
ViBe takes periodic snapshots for proctoring — it does not record continuous video. Snapshots are reviewed if a violation is flagged.

### 12.20 What is the single most common avoidable mistake learners make?
Switching to another tab or application during a ViBe session. The proctoring system registers this and issues a penalty. Keep the ViBe tab in focus at all times during a session.

### 12.21 Why does the lesson keep pausing or restarting even when I'm paying attention?
The most likely cause is a brief moment where your face was not visible to the camera (looking down at notes, moving away from the screen, etc.). Keep your face visible and centred in the camera view throughout the session.

### 12.22 Can I read the quiz questions aloud or mutter to myself while watching?
Yes — speaking to yourself is fine and does not trigger proctoring flags. Just ensure you remain the only person visible on camera.

### 12.23 Can I study with a friend on camera since we're learning together?
No. Multiple faces visible to the camera will trigger a proctoring flag. Study individually, even if you are in the same room as a friend.

### 12.24 Will I lose my progress if I clear my browser or reinstall it?
No — your progress is stored server-side, not in your browser. Clearing browser cache or reinstalling does not affect your course progress. You may need to log in again.

### 12.25 Is there a recommended daily learning rhythm on ViBe?
Complete one to two module segments per day rather than marathon sessions. This gives the system time to sync your progress and reduces the risk of proctoring fatigue (moving away from the camera, losing focus, etc.).

### 12.26 What should my "study corner" look like before I start a ViBe session?
- Single, well-lit room — no other people in camera view.
- Face clearly visible and centred in the webcam.
- ViBe tab as the only active tab (or at least in foreground).
- Stable internet connection.
- Headphones preferred to reduce audio distraction flags.
- Phone face-down and notifications silenced.

No. Teams must have the required number of members. A two-member team is not valid. You will need to find additional teammates before finalisation.

### 13.7 What if a team member leaves or becomes ineligible during Phase 1?
Report it to your mentor/admin via Yaksha chat immediately. The team will be adjusted accordingly.

### 13.8 Can I form a team with someone from my own college?
This is discouraged but not prohibited. The programme benefits from cross-institutional teams. If you have no alternative, it is allowed.

### 13.9 Can I form a team with students from my IIT MBS cohort?
Same answer as §13.8 — discouraged but not prohibited.

### 13.10 Can we change our team name after submission?
No. Team names are locked after submission.

### 13.11 What if multiple teams choose the same name?
Admin will contact the teams involved and ask one to choose a different name.

### 13.12 What should I do if I face issues within my team?
Raise it with your mentor first. If unresolved, escalate via Yaksha chat to admin.

### 13.13 How will I know who my mentor is?
Mentor details are shared via email and on your dashboard after team assignments are finalised.

### 13.14 When will I know my team details?
Team details are communicated via email after the team formation and assignment process is complete — typically within a few days of the formation activity.

### 13.15 I received a team list email but my name is not included. What should I do?
Contact admin immediately via Yaksha chat with your registered email and the email you received, so the discrepancy can be corrected.

### 13.16 We selected Project X as our top priority but were assigned Project Y. Can we change it?
Project assignments are final. The assignment process balances multiple factors across all teams and cannot accommodate individual reassignment requests.

### 13.17 I just started the internship. Can I form my own team now?
Team formation happens at defined windows during Phase 1. If you have just started, check your dashboard and Yaksha chat for the current status and next steps.

### 13.18 When do team activities begin?
Team activities (Phase 2 / Silver) begin after Phase 1 / Bronze is complete and teams have been assigned and oriented.

### 13.19 Can I request a specific teammate after teams are assigned?
No. Team assignments are final and **requests for changes are not entertained**.

### 13.20 What happens if a team member is inactive or not contributing?
You should **report the issue to your mentor/scholar early**. Prolonged inactivity may lead to administrative intervention.

### 13.21 Can I switch teams if there are conflicts?
Team switches are **not allowed** except in exceptional, admin-approved cases involving serious concerns.

### 13.22 Will team performance affect individual evaluation?
Yes. While some components may be individual, **team deliverables are a key part of evaluation**.

### 13.23 How will communication happen within teams?
Teams are expected to **self-organize internal team coordination** (e.g., a small WhatsApp/email thread limited to the four teammates). Official programme updates continue via email and Yaksha chat on samagama.in.

### 13.24 What if I miss the team allocation email?
All communication is sent via registered email. You are responsible for **checking regularly**, including spam folders.

### 13.25 Can a team be dissolved and reformed?
No. Once finalized, **teams are locked** and cannot be dissolved.

### 13.26 What happens if I drop out of the internship?
Your team will be adjusted accordingly, and the remaining members may continue as a **team of three or receive a replacement**.

### 13.27 Will we get time to get to know our teammates before Phase 2?
Yes. There is typically a **buffer period before Phase 2** where teams can connect and prepare.
